## 🛡️ PHISHING AWARENESS — `RECOGNIZE → AVOID → REPORT`

Fake MetaMask installers are one of the most common crypto scams. Knowing the patterns is your first line of defense.

- **Fake "AI Trading Agent" Campaigns** — HP Wolf Security documented malware that scans browsers for MetaMask and Coinbase Wallet extensions, then replaces them with malicious copies designed to steal credentials and drain wallets
- **Fake CAPTCHA Pages** — pages that ask you to complete a CAPTCHA before "installing" MetaMask are phishing attempts
- **Clipboard Hijackers** — malware that monitors your clipboard for wallet addresses and swaps them for the attacker's address. The legitimate MetaMask extension does not interact with your clipboard this way
- **Fake Mobile Apps** — Kaspersky identified 26 fraudulent apps impersonating MetaMask, Ledger, and Coinbase in app stores
- **Misspelled Domains** — `meatamaskk-chrm.gitbook.io` and similar impersonation pages exist to capture your login

`RECOGNIZE → AVOID → REPORT`

## 🔐 SEED PHRASE SAFETY — `PROTECT → NEVER SHARE → STORE OFFLINE`

Your seed phrase is the master key to your wallet. Anyone who has it can drain every account.

- **Never Enter Your Seed Phrase Online** — no website, no support agent, no "wallet validation" page needs it
- **Never Share It** — MetaMask support will never ask for your seed phrase
- **Store It Offline** — write it on paper or use a metal backup. Do not store it in a photo, cloud note, or password manager without encryption
- **Verify Before You Type** — if a page asks for your seed phrase, close it immediately

`PROTECT → NEVER SHARE → STORE OFFLINE`
